# Slice: Speak for me (AAC at work) — owner Alexandra
For autistic / non-speaking (always or under stress) users in meetings. No childish icons, no emojis. Adult, confident, professional communication.

## Implemented Architecture & Features

### 1. Speak for me Screen (`src/features/aac/SpeakForMeScreen.tsx`)
- **Quick phrases**: Adult, workplace-appropriate phrases ready for instant TTS playback without typing.
- **Personal custom phrases**: User-saved phrases persisted in `AsyncStorage` (`vibecheck_custom_phrases`), with add and delete controls.
- **Meeting prep bridge**: Phrases generated by `prepareMeeting` can be imported directly into personal quick phrases.
- **Text-to-speech**: Works on Expo Web (`window.speechSynthesis`) and Expo mobile (`expo-speech`), with speech speed adjustment (slow 0.8x, normal 0.95x, fast 1.1x).
- **Copy for chat**: Single-tap copy to clipboard with non-visual sensory confirmation (discreet sound & haptic vibration).

### 2. AI Contribution Composer (`functions/src/aac/composeContribution.ts`)
- **3 Specialized Modes**:
  1. `clarify`: Rewrites rough notes or questions into clear, direct, professional phrasing while preserving the user's exact stance.
  2. `calm`: Reframes potentially stressful questions or updates calmly and constructively to reduce workplace communication anxiety.
  3. `status_update`: Generates a crisp status update strictly from active tasks (done / in progress / blocked + unblocking needs).
- **Strict Guardrails**:
  - Strictly $\le 80$ words.
  - Zero health, medical, or capacity disclosure unless explicitly typed by the user.
  - Transparency: Every generation includes an `AiBadge` with a concise `why` explanation.
- **Deterministic fallback**: Rule-based templates activate automatically if Gemini API is unreachable, ensuring uninterrupted meeting assistance.
