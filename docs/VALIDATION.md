# User need & validation log

Goal: 3–5 short conversations (10 min) in the first 4 hours: mentors, people with chronic illness / neurodivergence, an HR person.

## Questions
1. Think of a bad-symptom day at work. What did you do with your schedule?
2. Have you ever asked for an adjustment? What stopped you / what helped?
3. Which would help most: adapting the day, tracking patterns, or help writing the request?
4. What would you NEVER want an app to share with your employer?
5. (HR) What does a good accommodation request look like?
